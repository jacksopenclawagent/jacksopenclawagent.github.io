Our Story

Started on the First Tee. Built for Every Tee.

First Teas was born from a simple idea on a grey Irish morning: there was no drink made specifically for golfers. Beer was too heavy. Water was too boring. Energy drinks were all wrong. So we made our own.

Founded in Ireland — where the greens are older than the game itself — First Teas is a hard tea with 5% ABV, naturally flavoured, and designed to be cracked open at the first tee box. Light enough to carry you through 18 holes. Good enough that you'll want another at the 19th.

We started small. Now we're shipping to golf clubs, sports bars, and pro shops around the world. The first tee looks a little different with a First Teas in hand.
